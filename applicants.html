Dear students:

Thank you for your interests in working with me. From time to time, I may have some PhD openings for potential applicants. I will regularly post funding opportunity here, or if you consider yourself self-funded or have other funding sources, please send me an email to discuss.

For all applicants, please follow the following procedure to submit your application:

  1. Go to the standard application web first: http://www.sussex.ac.uk/study/phd/apply

  2. When you fill the online form, you should enter the title of the scholarship in the finance section of the PhD application.

  3. Once you've done, please forward me your application ID and I will follow up your case and arrange an interview with you later.
